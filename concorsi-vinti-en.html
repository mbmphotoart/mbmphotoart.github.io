The story behind the photograph

Between sky and cinema.

In 2017, this photograph received an award in the Foto Garbani SA photography competition held during the 70th Locarno Film Festival.

The sky, with its moving clouds, meets the broad pale cinema screen. In a pared-back image, two meanings of time overlap: the changing weather above us and the anticipation before the film begins.
