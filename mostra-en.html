By Magda Bernasconi

“A photograph is a secret about a secret. The more it tells you, the less you know.”Diane Arbus
My vision

I love nature. In its presence I feel free, at home and at peace. Through photography I try to convey the feelings I experience while observing it.

Silence, scents and countless colours fascinate and carry me away. Travel becomes a reason to discover and photograph my world.

The exhibition photographs are printed entirely on glass: a choice that complements the project and invites you to look at each image slowly, making room for light and detail.
A photographic selection

Landscapes to listen to.

Places, water, forests and snow. Each photograph is a trace of a journey and a moment of silence.
