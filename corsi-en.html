My photographic journey

Every experience adds something to my way of seeing.
One attempt at a time.

01

Details

Light reveals every shape.

Gestures, lines and marks on skin become an exploration of form. Looking closely at the transition between light and shadow gives strength to composition.

  • Light and shadow
  • Shapes and gestures
  • Composition
02

Metamorphosis

Beyond first impressions.

A face, a gesture, a reflection: changing perspective to seek a different image. A visual exploration of identity, details and transformation.

  • Visual storytelling
  • Reflections
  • Expressive exploration
03

Portrait and atmosphere

Light meets emotion.

A gaze, a gesture, a detail: seeking expression through light and composition. Portraits exploring poses, framing and atmospheres, between delicacy and intensity.

  • Light and shadow
  • Pose and expression
  • Details
04

Body painting

The body becomes a canvas.

Colour, gesture and light meet in creative portraiture. A series of experiments in observing shapes, freezing movement and making room for details that distinguish each image.

  • Creative portraiture
  • Light and movement
  • Colour and details
05

Portraits

Presence before the pose.

In portraiture I seek the meeting of a person and the light. Expressions, shadows and small gestures become starting points for further observation.

  • Light on the face
  • Expression
  • Framing
06

Dance

The language of gestures.

Observe movement, capture a pause, make room for atmosphere. In dance, even a detail can tell the story of an encounter.

  • Movement
  • Shutter speed
  • Ambient light
07

Food

Light has a different flavour.

Food photography calls for careful construction: choosing a viewpoint, finding balance and letting light, shapes and colours tell the story.

  • Composition
  • Colour
  • Point of view
08

Light painting

Drawing in the dark.

When light becomes a mark, photography also means experimenting with time. A small workshop of imagination, exposure and movement.

  • Long exposures
  • Light trails
  • Experimentation
09

Colour powders

The energy of a moment.

Colour takes shape; movement leaves a trace. A chance to practise capturing the right instant and observe how light meets particles.

  • Timing
  • Movement
  • Colour and light
10

Tim Burton

Between dreams and unsettling wonder.

A photography workshop inspired by Tim Burton, the American film director and producer. Make-up, gestures and settings become tools for creating characters and atmospheres, exploring portraiture, colour and visual storytelling.

  • Creative portraiture
  • Atmosphere and colour
  • Storytelling through images
11

Woodland

Learning to see among the trees.

The woods offer an open-air workshop: filtered light, textures, reflections and movement. A journey in framing and finding new possibilities even in familiar subjects.

  • Natural light
  • Reflections
  • Creative movement
12

Environmental portrait

A presence changes a space.

Connecting a person with their surroundings: doors, rooms and natural light become part of the portrait. An exercise in composition that makes room for the figure and the atmosphere.

  • Natural light
  • Figure and surroundings
  • Composition
13

Stories in black and white

Let light and shadow speak.

A movement, a reflection, a backlit figure. Black and white invites us to observe contrast and shapes, seeking the instant when a scene becomes a story.

  • Black and white
  • Backlighting
  • Visual storytelling
14

Architecture and perspective

Follow the lines. Discover space.

Reflections, stairs and passages offer chances to train the eye. Black and white highlights geometry and contrast, while perspective guides the eye and gives the image depth.

  • Perspective
  • Black and white
  • Reflections and geometry
The journey continues

Technique can be learned.
Vision is cultivated.

I carry with me every experiment, every discovery and every new way of using light.
To return to photography with a little more awareness and the same curiosity.
